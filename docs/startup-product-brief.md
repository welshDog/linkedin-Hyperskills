# Startup product brief

## Executive summary

LinkedIn Hyperskills is a founder-first AI content operating system for personal branding on LinkedIn.

It helps founders and operators create high-signal, credible content without sounding like a generic AI tool. The product focuses on one wedge:

- learn the user’s voice
- generate content in that voice
- check the draft for trust and risk
- get approval before publishing

This is not a generic writing tool. It is a content intelligence system built for trust and authority.

## The problem

Founders and operators face a real challenge:

- they know LinkedIn matters
- they do not have time to write consistently
- generic AI writing sounds artificial
- writing in their own voice takes effort
- publishing without proof or strategy creates credibility risk
- most content tools optimize for volume instead of trust

The core pain is not “I need more content.”
The core pain is “I need content that sounds like me, and I need it to feel credible.”

## The solution

LinkedIn Hyperskills solves this with a simple loop:

1. Learn the owner’s brand memory
   - positioning
   - audience
   - tone and values
   - proof points
   - banned phrases and weak signals

2. Generate draft variants
   - 2-3 LinkedIn post options in the user’s style

3. Score and validate
   - clarity
   - hook strength
   - originality
   - trust and claim safety

4. Human approval
   - user edits, approves, or rejects before publishing

5. Publish and learn
   - track engagement and improve future drafts from evidence

## Why now

The market is ready because three things are happening at once:

- personal branding is now a business lever
- AI is now normal but still often generic
- founders are tired of writing in a way that feels forced or robotic

People want authentic leverage. LinkedIn is where that happens.

## Product positioning

The best positioning is:

AI content operating system for founder-led LinkedIn growth.

Not:
- AI ghostwriter
- generic marketing assistant
- another content generator

## Target users

Primary:
- founders
- operators
- builders
- B2B creators
- personal brands with a serious offer

Ideal early users:
- founders with a real product or service
- operators who need to build authority
- people who are already posting but want consistency and quality

## Market opportunity

The market is large and underserved:

- high-value founders and operators are already posting on LinkedIn
- many are dissatisfied with generic AI output
- many need a system to help them show up more consistently
- the opportunity is not just “writing” but trust-building and visibility

This is a product category opportunity, not just a feature opportunity.

## Differentiators

What makes this product different:

- brand memory instead of blank prompt state
- trust-first writing instead of generic copy
- performance feedback loop instead of one-off generation
- founder-focused positioning rather than generic marketing automation
- human approval before anything is published

## Business model

Early model:
- free tier for basic usage
- pro tier for individuals
- team tier for founders and operators
- enterprise layer for company-owned workflow

This is a SaaS product with founder-first monetization.

## Key metrics

Product metrics:
- time to first draft
- draft approval rate
- publish rate
- retention
- average post quality score

Business metrics:
- MRR
- conversion from free to paid
- retention at 30/60/90 days
- LTV

## The MVP message

The MVP is not trying to be everything.
It is trying to prove one thing:

Users can create credible LinkedIn content in their own voice with less friction and less effort.

## The mission

Help founders and operators build authentic, high-signal LinkedIn presence without sounding robotic.
