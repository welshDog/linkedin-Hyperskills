# LinkedIn Hyperskills — Startup Product Brief

## Executive Summary

**LinkedIn Hyperskills** is an AI-powered content operating system for founder-led personal branding.

It transforms how founders, operators, and personal brands approach LinkedIn growth by combining:

- AI-assisted content generation
- brand memory and voice consistency
- claim safety and trust verification
- performance analytics and feedback loops
- strategic playbooks for founders and operators
- campaign orchestration

Instead of producing generic AI content, it helps people sound like themselves while moving business objectives forward.

## The problem

**For founders and operators:**

- Writing takes time, but ghostwriting sounds fake
- LinkedIn algorithms favor consistent, high-signal content
- AI writing tools produce polished slop
- Personal branding requires strategy, not just posts
- Tracking what actually works is manual and fragmented
- Most tools optimize for volume, not signal

**For personal brands:**

- Building trust online requires authenticity
- One-off posts don't compound
- Engagement data rarely feeds back into better strategy
- Content systems are expensive and hard to manage
- AI writing risks sounding generic or making unverifiable claims

## The solution

LinkedIn Hyperskills is a content intelligence system that:

1. **Learns your voice** — stores your positioning, tone, proof points, and audience
2. **Generates smarter content** — writes in your voice, not in generic AI tone
3. **Verifies claims** — flags risky or unverifiable assertions before publishing
4. **Scores quality** — rates drafts on originality, strategic value, and trustworthiness
5. **Tracks performance** — captures engagement data and learns what works
6. **Orchestrates campaigns** — turns one idea into a content series
7. **Provides strategic modes** — founder, operator, builder, B2B SaaS, personal brand

## How it works

### User flow

1. **Onboard:** User uploads voice profile (5-10 past posts, bio, values)
2. **Brief:** User gives topic or goal
3. **Generate:** AI drafts content based on brand memory
4. **Score:** System flags claims, risks, and quality
5. **Review:** User sees a scored draft with recommendations
6. **Approve:** User approves, edits, or rejects
7. **Publish:** Schedule to LinkedIn via Publora or copy-paste
8. **Track:** System captures engagement and learns
9. **Improve:** Next draft is better based on real data
