# Launch plan

## Objective

Launch a tightly focused MVP that proves the wedge: founders can generate credible LinkedIn drafts in their own voice and review safety before publishing.

## Phase 1: Foundation and internal validation

### Goal
Ship a working product loop for a closed beta.

### Deliverables
- auth and onboarding
- profile creation
- sample post upload and voice extraction
- generation api
- score and safety checks
- approval workflow
- publishing fallback

### Success criteria
- users can sign up
- users can create a profile
- users can generate a post draft
- users can review the draft and approve
- generation and approval flow works in a live environment

## Phase 2: Closed beta

### Goal
Get 25-100 highly engaged users.

### Channels
- founder network
- product communities
- LinkedIn DM outreach
- niche founder communities
- personal social channels

### Metrics to track
- time to first draft
- percentage of drafts approved
- users who publish at least once
- quality score distribution
- user satisfaction and feedback

## Phase 3: Soft launch

### Goal
Prove the product has market pull and a clear user need.

### Tactics
- collect testimonials
- publish founder stories and use cases
- improve onboarding
- remove friction from the draft flow
- add a clearer copy-paste publishing fallback

## Phase 4: Public launch

### Goal
Drive signups and convert to paid users.

### Launch channels
- Product Hunt
- LinkedIn founder presence
- Twitter/X posts and threads
- newsletter and founder community outreach
- public launch page with waitlist

### Launch message

AI that sounds like you.
Not like a bot.

## Pricing model

- free tier for testing and onboarding
- pro tier for active individual users
- team tier for founders/operators working together
- enterprise later

## Risk management

Primary risks:
- poor authenticity in the generated drafts
- users dislike the copy quality
- claim safety is too weak
- onboarding friction is too high

Mitigations:
- ironclad human review before publish
- prompt tuning around voice and founder tone
- stronger safety heuristics and user warnings
- minimal onboarding to get to first value quickly

## Launch success metrics

- 1,000 signups in first wave
- 200 active users by month 2
- 10% conversion to paid
- 60%+ first-publish rate among activated users
- strong qualitative feedback: "this sounds like me"

## The launch story

The product story is simple and strong:

- founders need clearer, more credible content
- generic AI is not good enough
- the real opportunity is authentic founder voice at scale

That is why this product can create real value.
