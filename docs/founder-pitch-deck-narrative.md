## Slide 7-8: The solution

**LinkedIn Hyperskills is an AI content operating system that:**

1. **Learns your voice** (brand memory)
   - You upload 5-10 past posts
   - We extract your tone, positioning, proof points
   - Every generation uses this context

2. **Generates in your voice** (smart drafting)
   - AI writes 2-3 variants
   - Each sounds like you, not generic AI
   - Takes <30 seconds

3. **Verifies claims** (claim safety)
   - Flags risky or unverifiable assertions
   - Suggests rewrites
   - Protects your credibility

4. **Learns from results** (analytics loop)
   - Tracks what engages
   - Recommends next content
   - Gets smarter over time

## Deck mechanics

- **Total slides:** 30
- **Time:** 8-10 minutes
- **Style:** Confident, founder-focused, authentic
- **Tone:** Direct, no fluff, clear business logic
- **Visuals:** Product screenshots, metrics, market data

## Talking points by section

**Opportunity (Slides 1-2):**
- Set the scene: LinkedIn = business
- Introduce the tension: need + time + authenticity

**Problem (Slides 3-4):**
- Validate the problem with data
- Show the market is big and ready

**Solution (Slides 7-10):**
- Demo the product (if possible)
- Highlight what makes it different
- Reference the moat repeatedly

**Go-to-market (Slides 17-18):**
- Show founder-first strategy
- Reference existing founder validation
- Paint a clear path to scale

**Ask and close (Slides 23-30):**
- Clear number and use of funds
- Strong mission statement
- Reaffirm why this wins

**The pitch is not about features. It is about founder problems and why this team solves them.**
